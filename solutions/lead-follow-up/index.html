FAQ

Will automated messages sound robotic?

They shouldn’t. The messages are written in your voice, mention what the lead asked about, and every reply goes to a real person on your team.

Which lead sources can be connected?

Usually all of them: website forms, Facebook and Google lead forms, Instagram and Facebook messages, missed calls, and most booking tools. Anything that can send an email or a webhook can be connected.

Is it legal to text leads automatically?

Generally yes, when the lead gave consent, for example on your form, and every message includes an easy way to opt out. The setup includes consent wording and opt-out handling.

Do I need a new CRM?

Not necessarily. If you already use one, the follow-up is built around it. If your leads live in inboxes and spreadsheets, a simple CRM is usually part of the setup.
