FAQ

Does this work with my booking or practice software?

Often, yes. Many scheduling and practice management tools connect directly or through automation platforms. Where they can’t, reminders can run from a connected calendar.

How many reminders should we send?

Usually a confirmation at booking, a heads-up for visits booked far ahead, a two-way confirmation two days before and a short reminder on the day. More than that tends to annoy people.

Should we charge a no-show fee?

A clear policy helps with repeat no-shows, but most businesses get further with reminders and easy rescheduling first. Check your contracts and local rules before charging fees.

Can reminders include sensitive details?

They shouldn’t. Reminders stick to the date, time, location and how to confirm or reschedule, with no treatment or case details.
