Why do home service businesses miss so many jobs?

Because the people who answer the phone are usually up a ladder or under a sink. Urgent customers call the first company that picks up.

Missed calls on the job

You can't answer while you're working, and an urgent customer won't wait for a call back.

Quotes that go quiet

Estimates get sent and then forgotten, even though many customers just needed a nudge.

Busy and quiet seasons

Work floods in during peak season and dries up after, with no plan to fill the gaps.

Paying for leads that go nowhere

Lead sites and ads bring enquiries, but slow replies mean competitors win them.

How does automation help a home service business?

It replies to every call and form in seconds, books jobs and follows up on quotes automatically, so you win more work without spending evenings on the phone. It combines AI automation & AI chatbots, lead generation systems, Google Ads management, set up around the way your home services business already works.

Missed-call text back

Every missed call gets a text within seconds asking what they need and offering to book.

AI booking assistant

Answers common questions on your website and social pages, collects job details and photos, and books a visit.

Quote follow-up

Friendly automatic follow-ups after every estimate until the customer says yes or no.

Reminders & on-my-way texts

Appointment reminders and arrival updates that cut no-shows and missed visits.

Local search ads

Google Ads and Local Services Ads for urgent searches like "AC repair near me" or "emergency plumber".

Reviews & repeat work

Review requests after each job and seasonal reminders for maintenance and tune-ups.

What does this look like for a new customer?

  1. A homeowner's AC stops working on a Saturday and they call you while you're on another job.
  2. They instantly get a text: sorry we missed you, what's going on and where are you located?
  3. They reply with the problem and a photo, and pick a visit time from the link.
  4. They get a reminder before the visit and an on-my-way text on the day.
  5. After the job, they get a thank-you, a review request and later a maintenance reminder.
You stay in control: anything sensitive, complex or high-value is handed to you or your team, with the full conversation attached.

Case study: what does this look like in practice?

Client names are kept private. Here is how a similar system was set up for another business in this industry.

HVAC · Austin, Texas

Turning more inquiries into booked service calls

Scope: Google Ads, landing page, CRM and follow-up automation · Duration: 90 days

The business received inquiries while its technicians were out on jobs. The owner handled callbacks between service visits, and new leads were scattered across email, voicemail and a spreadsheet.

The system connects Google Ads to a landing page focused on residential HVAC services. An inquiry form collects the service needed, ZIP code and preferred callback time. Every submission creates a CRM record and sends an acknowledgment. The office receives a notification, while follow-up tasks flag inquiries awaiting contact. Staff confirm availability before booking a service visit.

Inquiry from ad→Acknowledgment sent→Office callback→Service call booked

Roofing · Denver, Colorado

Following up on estimate requests

Scope: Google Ads, estimate-request landing page and CRM automation · Duration: 12 weeks

The owner managed estimate requests alongside site visits and project supervision. Requests often lacked essential details, and follow-up depended on handwritten notes.

A focused landing page collects the property location, type of roofing request and preferred callback window. Optional photo uploads help staff understand the request before calling. The CRM gives each inquiry a status and creates follow-up tasks. Inspection reminders are sent once staff confirm a time with the homeowner.

Estimate request→Details and photos→Callback→Inspection scheduled

FAQ

Does missed-call text back work with my business number?

In most cases, yes. It can usually be added to your existing number through your phone provider or a business texting tool, so customers keep calling the number they already know.

Can it give prices or quotes?

It can share starting prices or ranges you approve and collect the details and photos you need to quote. Final quotes stay with you, so nothing is promised that you haven't signed off on.

Will it work with my scheduling or field service software?

Often, yes. Many field service tools can connect directly or through automation platforms. If not, bookings can go into a calendar with an alert to you or your office.
