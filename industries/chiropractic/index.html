Why do chiropractic and PT clinics lose new patients?

Because people in pain call several clinics at once, and the front desk can't answer while it's busy with the patients already in the room.

Calls missed at the front desk

Check-ins, payments and insurance questions keep the desk busy, so new-patient calls go to voicemail.

Patients who drop off mid-plan

Patients feel better after a few visits, stop booking, and never finish the care plan that would have helped.

No-shows and late cancellations

Empty slots cost time you can't get back, and manual reminder calls take hours each week.

Leads that never get a reply

Website forms and ad leads sit in an inbox until the next day, by which time the patient booked elsewhere.

FAQ

Is this HIPAA compliant?

Chiropractic and PT clinics are generally covered by HIPAA, so the setup is built to keep health details out of open texts and uses tools that will sign a Business Associate Agreement where patient information is handled. Your own compliance review still applies, and no automation is a compliance guarantee on its own.

Will patients know they're talking to an AI?

The assistant is set up to be clear about what it is, and it hands anything clinical or sensitive to your team. Most patients care that they get a fast, helpful answer and an easy way to book.

Does it work with our scheduling or EHR software?

Often, yes. Many practice management and scheduling tools can connect directly or through automation platforms. Where they can't, bookings can go to a calendar with an alert to the front desk.
