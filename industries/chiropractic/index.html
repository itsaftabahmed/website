Why do chiropractic and PT clinics lose new patients?

Because people in pain call several clinics at once, and the front desk can't answer while it's busy with the patients already in the room.

Calls missed at the front desk

Check-ins, payments and insurance questions keep the desk busy, so new-patient calls go to voicemail.

Patients who drop off mid-plan

Patients feel better after a few visits, stop booking, and never finish the care plan that would have helped.

No-shows and late cancellations

Empty slots cost time you can't get back, and manual reminder calls take hours each week.

Leads that never get a reply

Website forms and ad leads sit in an inbox until the next day, by which time the patient booked elsewhere.

How does automation help a chiropractic or PT clinic?

It answers new patients instantly, books the first visit, and sends reminders and rebooking prompts automatically, so your team spends its time on care instead of the phone. It combines AI automation & AI chatbots, lead generation systems, Google Ads management, set up around the way your chiropractic & pt clinic already works.

Missed-call text back

Every unanswered call gets a friendly text within seconds offering to book a first visit.

AI booking assistant

Answers common questions about first visits, hours and insurance on your website and social pages, and books into your schedule.

Reminders & confirmations

Text and email reminders before every visit, with an easy way to confirm or reschedule.

Care-plan follow-up

Gentle prompts when a patient hasn't booked their next visit, so fewer people drop off halfway.

Local search ads

Google Ads for high-intent searches like "chiropractor near me" or "physical therapy for back pain".

Reviews & reactivation

Review requests after visits and check-ins with past patients who haven't been in for a while.

What does this look like for a new patient?

  1. Someone hurts their back lifting boxes on a Sunday and searches for a chiropractor near them.
  2. They find your clinic, call, and get a text back within seconds because the line is busy.
  3. The assistant answers a question about the first visit and offers Monday morning, which they book.
  4. They get a reminder with directions and what to bring the day before.
  5. After a few visits, if they don't book the next one, they get a friendly nudge to keep their plan on track.
You stay in control: anything sensitive, complex or high-value is handed to you or your team, with the full conversation attached.

FAQ

Is this HIPAA compliant?

Chiropractic and PT clinics are generally covered by HIPAA, so the setup is built to keep health details out of open texts and uses tools that will sign a Business Associate Agreement where patient information is handled. Your own compliance review still applies, and no automation is a compliance guarantee on its own.

Will patients know they're talking to an AI?

The assistant is set up to be clear about what it is, and it hands anything clinical or sensitive to your team. Most patients care that they get a fast, helpful answer and an easy way to book.

Does it work with our scheduling or EHR software?

Often, yes. Many practice management and scheduling tools can connect directly or through automation platforms. Where they can't, bookings can go to a calendar with an alert to the front desk.
