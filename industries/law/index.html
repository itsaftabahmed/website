Where do law firms lose potential clients?

At intake. Calls go to voicemail, web forms wait until the next business day, and potential clients keep searching until someone responds.

Calls to voicemail

Attorneys are in court or with clients, and callers rarely leave a message.

Slow web-form replies

Forms submitted at night or on weekends wait until Monday, by which time the person has hired someone else.

Time spent on poor-fit enquiries

Staff spend hours on calls about practice areas or locations the firm doesn't handle.

Unclear marketing results

It's hard to tell which ads and sources produce signed cases, not just calls.

How can a law firm automate client intake?

With an intake assistant that responds instantly, asks your screening questions, books consultations for good-fit cases and routes everything else politely, without ever giving legal advice. It combines AI automation & AI chatbots, Google Ads management, lead generation systems, set up around the way your law firm firm already works.

24/7 intake assistant

Answers on your website and by text, collects contact details and the basics of the matter, and explains next steps.

Case screening

Asks your screening questions (practice area, location, timing) and flags good-fit cases to you immediately.

Consultation booking

Qualified prospects book a consultation in your calendar, with reminders and easy rescheduling.

Missed-call text back

Callers who reach voicemail get a text within seconds so the conversation continues.

Practice-area search ads

Google Ads for specific case types and locations, with landing pages written for each.

Source tracking

Every lead tagged by source so you can see which marketing produces consultations and signed cases.

What does this look like for a new client?

  1. Someone searches for a lawyer for their type of case late in the evening and finds your page.
  2. Your intake assistant responds immediately and asks a few screening questions.
  3. Their matter fits your practice, so they book a consultation from your calendar.
  4. You get a summary of the enquiry, and they get reminders before the appointment.
  5. If the matter isn't a fit, they get a polite reply and, if you like, a referral resource.
You stay in control: anything sensitive, complex or high-value is handed to you or your team, with the full conversation attached.

FAQ

Will the AI give legal advice?

No. It is set up only to handle intake: collecting contact details and basic facts, answering general questions about your firm and booking consultations. It makes clear that no attorney-client relationship is formed and that legal questions go to an attorney.

What about confidentiality?

The intake assistant collects only what's needed to screen and book, and the tools are chosen with confidentiality in mind. Anything sensitive is handed to your team, and the setup is reviewed with you before launch.

Does it follow attorney advertising rules?

Ads and intake messages are written to avoid guarantees and misleading claims. Advertising rules vary by state, so final wording is reviewed by you before anything goes live.
