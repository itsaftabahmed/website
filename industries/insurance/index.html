Why do insurance agencies lose quote requests?

Because shoppers contact several agencies at once, and requests from forms, ads and phone calls land in different places with no one clearly responsible for the first reply.

Slow replies to quote requests

Agents are on calls or in appointments, so web and ad leads wait hours while the shopper hears from other agencies.

Missing details

Requests arrive without the coverage type, vehicle or property details, so the first call is spent gathering basics instead of quoting.

Leads with no clear owner

Requests sit in a shared inbox or an agent's personal notes, and nobody can see which ones were never contacted.

Renewals that slip by

Without reminders, clients reach their renewal date without a review, and some shop around and leave.

What does this look like for a new client?

  1. A homeowner gets a renewal notice with a higher premium and searches for a local insurance agent on a weekday evening.
  2. They fill in a quote form on your site and get a text within seconds confirming you received it.
  3. The text asks three quick questions about the property and their renewal date, then offers times to talk with an agent.
  4. They book a call for the next morning, and the assigned agent sees the details in the CRM before dialing.
  5. After they become a client, a reminder goes out ahead of next year's renewal so the agent can review coverage first.
You stay in control: anything sensitive, complex or high-value is handed to you or your team, with the full conversation attached.

FAQ

Can the AI give quotes or coverage advice?

No. It is set up for administrative tasks: replying to requests, collecting basic details, answering general questions about your agency and booking calls. Coverage recommendations and quotes come from your licensed agents, in line with your state's licensing rules.

What about consent rules for texting and calling leads?

Automated texts and calls are regulated under the TCPA and state laws, so forms are set up to collect clear consent, messages include opt-out instructions, and replies go only to people who contacted you. Your own compliance review and any carrier marketing rules still apply.

Does it work with our agency management system or CRM?

Often, yes. Many agency management systems and CRMs can connect directly or through automation platforms. Where they can't, leads can go to a simple CRM or shared calendar with alerts to your team.
