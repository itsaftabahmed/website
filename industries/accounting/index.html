What slows accounting and CPA firms down the most?

Repetitive admin. Answering the same questions, onboarding new clients by hand and chasing missing documents take time away from billable work, and peak season makes it worse.

The same questions, every day

"What do you charge?", "Do you handle my type of return?", "What documents do I need?" asked again and again.

Manual onboarding

Engagement letters, intake forms and welcome emails sent one by one, so new clients wait.

Chasing documents

Hours spent emailing clients for missing receipts, statements and signatures.

Unpredictable new clients

Growth relies on referrals, and enquiries spike in tax season and dry up after.

How can an accounting firm use AI automation?

By automating the steps that repeat for every client (enquiries, intake, reminders and follow-up) while your team keeps control of anything that needs professional judgment. It combines AI automation & AI chatbots, Google Ads management, lead generation systems, set up around the way your accounting & cpa firm already works.

AI enquiry assistant

Answers routine questions about services, process and fees on your website, and qualifies new enquiries.

Consultation booking

Qualified prospects book a discovery call straight into your calendar, with reminders.

Automated onboarding

Engagement letter, intake form and welcome sequence go out automatically once a client says yes.

Document reminders

Friendly automatic reminders until every document is in, with a clear checklist for the client.

Year-round lead generation

Google Ads and landing pages for the services you want more of, such as bookkeeping, small-business tax or advisory.

Client reactivation

Timely messages to past clients before tax season and for year-round services.

What does this look like for a new client?

  1. A small-business owner searches for "bookkeeping for small business" and lands on your page.
  2. Your assistant answers their questions and asks about their business type, size and needs.
  3. They book a consultation, and you get a summary of the enquiry before the call.
  4. Once they sign up, onboarding starts automatically: engagement letter, intake form and welcome email.
  5. Document reminders go out on a schedule until everything is in, without anyone chasing by hand.
You stay in control: anything sensitive, complex or high-value is handed to you or your team, with the full conversation attached.

FAQ

Is it safe to use AI with client financial information?

The assistant is set up to handle enquiries, scheduling and reminders, not to collect sensitive financial data in chat. Documents still go through your secure client portal, and the tools are chosen to fit your firm's security and confidentiality requirements.

Will the AI give tax advice to clients?

No. It answers routine questions about your services and process and hands anything that needs professional judgment to your team. That keeps advice where it belongs and saves your staff time on the basics.

Does it work with the software we already use?

Usually, yes. Automation is built around your existing tools, such as your CRM or practice management system, client portal, e-signature and calendar, rather than forcing you to switch.
