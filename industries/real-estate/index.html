Why do real estate agents lose so many leads?

Mostly because of speed and follow-up. Leads come in at all hours from many sources, and the ones that aren't ready to move this week are often forgotten.

Slow first response

A lead comes in while you're at a showing or a closing, and by the time you call back they've spoken to another agent.

Leads scattered everywhere

Portal leads, Facebook leads, website forms and open-house sign-ins all live in different places.

"Just looking" leads go cold

Most people are months away from moving, and manual follow-up stops after a call or two.

Time spent on tire-kickers

Hours go into calls with people who aren't qualified, pre-approved or serious yet.

How does AI follow-up work for real estate agents?

Every new lead gets an instant, personal reply, a few qualifying questions and an easy way to book, and then stays on a follow-up plan until they're ready. It combines AI automation & AI chatbots, lead generation systems, Facebook & Instagram ads, set up around the way your real estate & property business already works.

Instant lead response

New leads from portals, ads and your website get a text and email within seconds, in your voice.

AI qualification

Asks about buying or selling, area, budget, timeline and financing, and flags hot leads to you right away.

Showing & call booking

Offers times from your calendar for a call, valuation or showing, with confirmations and reminders.

Long-term nurture

Helpful, low-pressure check-ins and new listing alerts for leads who aren't ready yet.

One CRM for every source

Every lead in one place with its source, status and next step, so nothing slips through.

Listing & seller ads

Facebook, Instagram and Google campaigns for listings, home valuations and open houses.

What does this look like for a new lead?

  1. Someone fills in a home-valuation form from your Facebook ad on a Sunday evening.
  2. Within seconds they get a personal text from your assistant thanking them and asking a couple of quick questions.
  3. They answer: selling in 3 to 6 months, already found their next area. You get an alert marking it a hot lead.
  4. They book a call slot from your calendar right in the conversation.
  5. If they're not ready yet, they get useful updates over the coming months instead of being forgotten.
You stay in control: anything sensitive, complex or high-value is handed to you or your team, with the full conversation attached.

Keeping property inquiries moving

Scope: Property landing pages, CRM routing and showing follow-up · Duration: 8 weeks

The agency's agents received property inquiries through several channels. Requests were forwarded manually, and agents sometimes lacked a clear record of which listing a prospect was interested in.

Property landing pages pass the listing reference and inquiry details into the CRM. Each request is assigned to an agent and triggers an acknowledgment. Agents receive follow-up tasks, record contact attempts and arrange showings. Confirmed showing details feed into reminder messages.

FAQ

Can the AI reply to Zillow and Realtor.com leads?

In most cases, yes. Portal leads usually arrive by email or through your CRM, and the automation can pick them up and reply by text and email within seconds. The exact setup depends on which portals and CRM you use.

Will leads know they're talking to an assistant?

The assistant is set up to be clear that it's helping on your behalf and to sound natural and friendly. As soon as a lead wants to talk to you or is clearly serious, it hands over with the full conversation attached.

Does this replace my CRM?

Not necessarily. If you already use a real estate CRM, the automation is built around it. If your leads are scattered across inboxes and spreadsheets, setting up a simple CRM is usually part of the first step.
