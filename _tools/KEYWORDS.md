# Keyword plan (researched 2026-11-05)

Who searches: US small-business owners looking for help (not their customers). Their searches are small in number but high in intent, so a page ranking for a 300-a-month phrase can still bring clients.

Volume figures are public estimates from third-party sources (ranges, not exact). Replace them with Google Keyword Planner / Search Console data when the owner shares it.

## Priority 1: AI receptionist (fastest-growing topic)
- Short tail: "ai receptionist" (about 4,400 to 5,400 US searches a month in mid-2026 and rising fast; DataForSEO/Google Ads estimates cited by findskill.ai and credminds.com). Very competitive (vendors).
- Long tail to target with posts and industry pages: ai receptionist cost / pricing, ai receptionist for dental office, for law firms, for hvac, for real estate, for med spa, virtual receptionist vs ai receptionist, best ai receptionist for small business, hipaa compliant ai receptionist, ai receptionist vs answering service (published).
- Pages: services/ai-automation, industry pages (dental and chiropractic titles already use "AI receptionist").

## Priority 2: Industry marketing phrases (owner-intent, low competition)
- "med spa advertising" (about 1,300/mo), "med spa marketing" (about 590/mo), "med spa marketing agency" (390), "med spa seo" (320) (Keyword Planner figures cited by enrichlabs.ai, 2026).
- "real estate lead generation" (about 9,900/mo per a 2026 real estate keyword guide), plus "facebook ads for real estate agents" and "best crm for real estate agents".
- Same pattern for each industry: "<industry> marketing", "<industry> lead generation", "google ads for <industry>", "facebook ads for <industry>". All are in blog/topics.md.

## Priority 3: Problem searches (long tail, easiest to rank)
- Examples: facebook leads not answering, google ads clicks no conversions, website traffic but no leads, reduce appointment no shows, missed call text back vs voicemail. These bring owners who already feel the pain.

## Short tail to avoid chasing directly
- "google ads management", "facebook ads agency", "lead generation", "ai automation": huge competition from big agencies and software companies. Rank for them indirectly through service pages plus many long-tail posts linking to them.

## Rules
- One main phrase per page; never two pages targeting the same phrase.
- Put the phrase in the title, H1, first paragraph, one H2 and the meta description, naturally.
- Re-check this plan monthly in the SEO routine against Search Console queries (positions 8 to 30 are refresh candidates).
