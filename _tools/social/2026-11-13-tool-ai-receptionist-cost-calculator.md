## LinkedIn
The cheapest AI receptionist plan on the pricing page is often not the cheapest for your business.

I built a free calculator that prices your own call volume on the four ways these tools are billed:
- Flat monthly
- Per minute
- Per call
- Bundle plus overage

Enter your calls a month and average call length, type in the rates you were quoted, and it shows which plan type is lowest, in a normal month and in your busiest month.

No sign-up. Example rates are labelled as examples.
