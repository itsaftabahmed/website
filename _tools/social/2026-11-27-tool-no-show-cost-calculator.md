## LinkedIn
Every empty appointment slot has a price. Most practices and service businesses have never added it up.

So I built a free calculator:
- Enter booked appointments per week, your no-show rate and what a visit is worth
- It accounts for the no-shows who rebook later, so the number is honest
- It shows the monthly and yearly cost, and what cutting no-shows would win back
