# How to reduce no-shows at a dental office
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/reduce-dental-no-shows/

## LinkedIn
Most dental no-shows aren't patients who don't care.

They forgot. The time stopped working. Or rescheduling meant calling during work hours, so not showing up was easier.

Those are memory and friction problems, and they're fixable:
- Confirm at booking with an add-to-calendar link
- Send a two-way text 2 days before: reply C to confirm, R to reschedule
- Short reminder on the morning of the visit
- When someone cancels, auto-text a short-notice waitlist. First reply gets the slot.

The two-day confirmation is the key. It tells you on Tuesday that Thursday's 10:30 needs filling, while you still have time to fill it.
