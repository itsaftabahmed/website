## LinkedIn
"How much does an AI receptionist cost?" The plan price is the wrong place to start. Your call volume is.

What decides the bill:
- Self-serve plans typically run about $30 to $300 a month
- Four billing models: flat, per minute, per call, and a bundle with overage
- In my hypothetical 300-call example, the plan with the lowest advertised price cost about twice the flat plan
- Extras add up: setup, extra numbers, texting, connections
- Compare the fee with the calls you miss today, not with zero
