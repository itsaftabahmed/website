# How med spas can reduce consultation no-shows
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/med-spa-no-shows/

## LinkedIn
Most med spa no-shows aren't rude clients. They're impulse bookings from an Instagram ad that never heard enough from the clinic to stay committed.

What closes the gap between booking and showing up:
- A small deposit or card on file, explained as a booking hold and credited to treatment
- Reminders that ask for a reply (confirm or reschedule), not just the time
- A short pre-visit message: who they'll meet, what happens, no pressure to buy
- An automatic waitlist that refills late cancellations

And track no-shows by lead source. Ad leads and referrals behave very differently.
