# AI receptionist pricing models compared: per minute vs flat rate vs per call
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/ai-receptionist-pricing-models/

## LinkedIn
Two AI receptionist plans can quote the same "starting at" price and end up hundreds of dollars apart. The pricing model decides it.

Quick break-even math (example rates):
- Flat vs per minute: flat price ÷ per-minute rate = the minutes where flat wins
- Per call vs per minute: per-call price ÷ per-minute rate = the call length where per call wins
- Bundles look cheap until a busy month hits the overage
- Check billing increments: rounding up to the full minute can add close to 40%
