## LinkedIn
"Facebook leads are junk" usually means three things went wrong: a vague offer, a form that asks nothing, and follow-up that starts tomorrow.

What works better for agents:
- Accept the housing ad limits and let the ad copy do the targeting
- Use offers only serious buyers care about (a specific listing, a first-time buyer plan)
- Use a higher intent lead form with 2 or 3 questions: timeframe, pre-approval, area
- Text within minutes, then call
- Judge ads by booked showings, not cost per lead
