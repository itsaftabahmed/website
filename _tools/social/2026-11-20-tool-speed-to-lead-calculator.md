## LinkedIn
How much is your reply time costing you?

Most small businesses never put a number on it. So I built a free calculator that does:
- Enter your leads per month and how many get a reply within 5 minutes
- Add your close rate on fast replies and on slow ones, plus your average job value
- It shows how many leads wait too long and what answering faster is worth each month and each year

No sign-up, and you can share your result with a link.
