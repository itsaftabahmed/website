# Missed call text back: never lose a caller again
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/missed-call-text-back/

## LinkedIn
When a call to your business goes unanswered, the caller doesn't wait. They call the next business.

Missed call text back is the simplest fix I know:
- Unanswered call, then a text from your business number within seconds
- One short question: "Sorry we missed you, what can we help with?"
- The caller replies when it suits them, instead of trying the next number
- It usually runs on the number customers already know

Two things to get right in the US: register your number for business texting (A2P 10DLC) and handle STOP replies automatically.

And the part most setups skip: someone (or an AI assistant) has to answer the reply fast.

Full guide with first-text examples by industry: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/missed-call-text-back/
