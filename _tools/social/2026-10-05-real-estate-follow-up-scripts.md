## LinkedIn
Most real estate follow-up texts ask for too much.

"Let me know if you'd like to schedule a showing or discuss your home search!" gives the lead nothing to answer.

Compare: "Hi Mark, saw you looked at 42 Oak St. Are you hoping to move this year or just getting a feel for prices?"

What makes a follow-up script work:
- Speed: minutes, not hours
- One personal detail (the address, the area)
- One easy question
- A reason to reply (similar homes, price history)

And when a lead goes quiet, ask "Should I keep sending homes, pause, or stop?" It gets more honest replies than any pitch.
