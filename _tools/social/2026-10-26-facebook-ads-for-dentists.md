## LinkedIn
"Facebook ads only bring bargain hunters who never show up." I hear this from dentists a lot. Usually it's the campaign, not the platform.

What works for dental practices:
- Promote treatments people plan: new-patient exams, whitening, aligners, implants
- Write about the offer, not the viewer's teeth (Meta bans ads that imply health conditions)
- Keep patient health data out of the Meta Pixel
- Use lead forms with 2-3 qualifying questions and the higher-intent form type
- Reply within minutes and offer real appointment times

The follow-up decides more of the result than the ad does.

Full guide with a worked budget example: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/facebook-ads-for-dentists/
