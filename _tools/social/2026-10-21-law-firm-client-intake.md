# Law firm client intake: how to stop losing potential cases
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/law-firm-client-intake/

## LinkedIn
Most law firms don't lose cases because of their lawyering. They lose them at intake, before an attorney hears about them.

Where potential cases slip away:
- Calls that ring out during court, lunch and weekends
- Web forms that wait until the next business day
- Every staff member screening with different questions
- No follow-up after the consultation

What fixes it: an immediate response to every inquiry, one intake script per practice area, a conflict check before detailed facts, and consultation booking built in. Attorneys keep every judgment call.
