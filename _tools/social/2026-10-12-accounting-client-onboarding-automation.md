# How to automate client onboarding at an accounting firm
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/accounting-client-onboarding-automation/

## LinkedIn
Most accounting firms don't lose time on the work. They lose it chasing paperwork from new clients.

Onboarding is the same sequence every time, which makes it easy to automate:
- Engagement letter goes out for e-signature the moment a client says yes
- Signing triggers an intake form and a document checklist that matches their situation
- Reminders list only what's still missing, so nobody chases by hand
- The kickoff call books itself once the essentials are in

One rule: collect documents through a secure portal, never plain email. Tax firms are covered by the FTC Safeguards Rule.

Many firms already pay for tools that do all of this. The features just aren't switched on and connected.

Full guide with a one-week setup plan: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/accounting-client-onboarding-automation/
