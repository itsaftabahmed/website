# Missed calls are costing your HVAC business jobs
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/hvac-missed-calls/

## LinkedIn
When an AC dies on a 95-degree evening, the homeowner calls companies in order until someone answers.

If your line rings out, most of them won't leave a voicemail. They call the next company.

What I see with HVAC owners:
- The most calls get missed in heat waves, cold snaps, evenings and weekends, exactly when demand peaks
- Many of those callers came from ads or Local Services Ads, so you paid for the call
- A repair visit is often how a customer meets the company that later replaces their system
- "Answer harder" stops working the moment the busy season starts

The fix: a text back within seconds of every missed call, one triage question, and a way to book the visit straight from the conversation.

Try this: pull last month's unanswered-call report from your phone provider and look at the hours.
