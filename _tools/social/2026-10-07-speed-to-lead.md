# Speed to lead: why the first 5 minutes decide the sale
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/speed-to-lead/

## LinkedIn
The best follow-up message in the world loses to an average one sent first.

Most people who need a plumber, a dentist or an agent contact two or three businesses at once. Whoever replies first with something useful usually gets the job.

What slows small businesses down is rarely laziness:
- You're with a customer when the lead comes in
- Leads arrive in the evening or at the weekend
- Forms, ad leads and DMs live in different inboxes
- Nobody owns the first reply

The fix is a system, not willpower: one inbox for every lead, an instant first reply that asks one easy question, and an alert when someone is ready to book.

Try this: fill in your own website form at 8pm on a Saturday and see how long it takes to hear back.
