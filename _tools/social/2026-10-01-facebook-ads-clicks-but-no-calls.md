# Why your Facebook ads get clicks but no calls
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/facebook-ads-clicks-but-no-calls/

## LinkedIn
"My Facebook ads get clicks, but the phone doesn't ring."

I hear this from business owners all the time, and the problem is almost never the targeting.

It's usually what happens after the click:
1. The offer is vague, so there's no reason to act today
2. The page is slow or confusing on a phone
3. The form asks too much, or too little
4. Nobody follows up for hours (or days)

That last one is the silent killer. A lead who filled in a form at 8pm and hears nothing until tomorrow has already called someone else.

I wrote up the six most common causes and a 7-day plan to fix them:
https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/facebook-ads-clicks-but-no-calls/
