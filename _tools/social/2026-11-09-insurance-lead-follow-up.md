# How insurance agents can follow up on quote requests faster
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/insurance-lead-follow-up/

## LinkedIn
Insurance shoppers request quotes from several agencies at once. The first useful reply usually wins the conversation.

What works:
- Automatic text and email within a minute, from a named agent
- Ask only coverage type, ZIP, renewal date and best time to call
- Assign every lead to one agent, with an alert if nobody opens it in 15 minutes
- About 10 days of follow-up, then a reminder before their renewal date
