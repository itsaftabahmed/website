# How to follow up with leads automatically (SMS, email, calls)
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/automated-lead-follow-up/

## LinkedIn
Most service businesses don't lose leads to bad ads. They lose them after the first unanswered call.

A follow-up setup that works:
- Text and email within a minute, plus an alert for someone to call
- About 6 to 8 touches over 14 days across text, email and calls
- A final "should I close your request?" text (often the one people answer)
- Stop rules: any reply, booking or STOP ends the sequence
- Consent on your forms and a registered texting number

Full guide with the 14-day sequence: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/automated-lead-follow-up/
