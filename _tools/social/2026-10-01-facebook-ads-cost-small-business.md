# How much do Facebook ads cost for a small business?
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/facebook-ads-cost-small-business/

## LinkedIn
"How much do Facebook ads cost?" is the wrong question.

You set the budget. Meta runs an auction. What you get for that money depends on your industry, location, offer and creative.

The question that actually matters: what does it cost you to get a customer from Facebook, and is that profitable?

Work backwards:
- What is a customer worth to you?
- What can you afford to pay to win one?
- How many leads does it take to get one customer?

Then set a test budget you can keep running for 2 to 3 months, and judge it on cost per booking, not cost per click.
