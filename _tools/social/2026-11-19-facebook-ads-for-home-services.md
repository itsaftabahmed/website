# Facebook ads for home service businesses (HVAC, plumbing, roofing)

## LinkedIn
"Facebook ads don't work for contractors." They do, for the right jobs.

What works for HVAC, plumbing and roofing:
- Facebook for planned jobs (tune-ups, maintenance plans, replacements, inspections), Google for emergencies
- A specific local offer beats a generic discount
- 2 or 3 qualifying questions in the form (own the home? what job? when?)
- An automatic text within a minute, then a call
- Judge it on booked jobs, not cost per lead
