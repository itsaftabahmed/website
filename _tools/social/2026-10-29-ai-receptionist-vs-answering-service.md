## LinkedIn
AI receptionist or live answering service? It depends on your calls.

Mostly routine (hours, prices, booking)? AI usually wins: instant answers and the call ends with a booked time, not a message.

Mostly urgent, emotional or unusual? People still handle those better.

The setup I recommend for many service businesses: AI first, clear handoff to a person, every call logged in your CRM.

Quick test: sort your last 50 calls into routine vs judgment calls. The bigger pile tells you where to start.
