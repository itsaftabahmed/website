# How chiropractors can get more new patients without discounts

## LinkedIn
A $29 first visit fills a chiropractor's calendar for a week. Then the follow-ups don't come.

Discounts shape who shows up. What brings patients who stay for their care plan:
- Answer fast. People in pain book with the first clinic that picks up
- A Google profile with recent reviews that mention real problems solved
- Google search ads for "chiropractor near me", not broad discount offers
- A simple referral ask when patients say they feel better
- Check-ins with past patients at 90 days and six months

Try this: call your own clinic at 5:30 pm from an unknown number and see what a new patient gets.
