# How to measure your lead response time (and what a good one is)
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/lead-response-time/

## LinkedIn
Most business owners think they reply to leads fast. The numbers often disagree.

How to check in 30 minutes:
- Take your last 20 to 30 leads
- Note when each arrived and when someone first replied
- Look at the median and the share answered within 5 minutes, not the average
- Check evenings, weekends and ad leads separately

A good target: an automatic reply within a minute and a person within 5 minutes.
