# AI receptionists: answering calls and booking appointments 24/7
Link: https://iamaftabahmed.com/blog/ai-receptionist-small-business/

## LinkedIn
An AI receptionist isn't a replacement for your best front desk person. It's a way to make sure no caller ever reaches voicemail again.

What it does well:
- Answers calls and texts 24/7 with your approved answers
- Books, reschedules and cancels using your real calendar
- Asks intake questions and saves them to your CRM
- Hands urgent, sensitive or unusual calls to a person fast

What it shouldn't do: give professional advice, promise what you haven't approved, or pretend to be human.

Best place to start: after-hours and overflow calls. Those are calls you're already losing.
