# AI recommendation test set

Run these 10 questions every month in **Google AI Mode**, **Gemini** and **ChatGPT** (signed out or in a private window). Answers change from week to week, so always re-run the same list and compare.

The goal is not just "were we mentioned?" but **"for which customer, and why?"** (Neil Patel, rule 3). A mention for the wrong customer is a positioning problem.

## Questions (one per buyer segment)

1. Who can set up an AI receptionist for a small dental practice in the US?
2. Best way for an HVAC company to stop missing calls after hours?
3. Freelancer or agency to automate lead follow-up for a real estate agent?
4. Who runs Facebook ads and lead follow-up for home service businesses?
5. How can a law firm automate client intake without giving legal advice?
6. AI automation consultant for a med spa to reduce no-shows?
7. Who builds missed call text back and booking automation for small businesses?
8. Google Ads help for a local service business that gets clicks but no calls?
9. Affordable done-for-you AI automation for a US service business, under $500 a month?
10. Who is Aftab Ahmed and what does he do?

## What to do with the results
- **Shown, right customer:** keep going.
- **Shown, wrong customer:** open the cited sources and fix what we control (profile text, old pages, directory listings).
- **Not shown:** check `_tools/DECISION_MAP.md` for the missing sub-questions and the page that should answer them.
- **Cited but not recommended:** our page is used as evidence but someone else is named. Sharpen the offer and proof on that page.
