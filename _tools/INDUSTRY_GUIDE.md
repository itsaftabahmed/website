# Automated industry pages: what to do on each run

Industry pages are sales pages for one type of business (`/industries/<slug>/`). They are generated by `_tools/gen_industries.py`; never hand-edit the generated HTML, edit the `I` list in the script and re-run it.

Audience: US small-business owners in that industry. Voice: Aftab Ahmed, plain, direct, practical, no hype. Follow the "Quality rules" in `blog/WRITING_GUIDE.md` (no filler phrases, no em dashes, specific over vague).

## Every run

1. `git fetch origin main && git merge origin/main` first.
2. If an unchecked industry is in the queue: do **A. New industry page**. Otherwise do **B. Refresh**.
3. On a run that falls on the 1st of the month, also do **B. Refresh** for one existing page (in addition to A).
4. Test, commit and push (see **Finish**).

## A. New industry page

1. Add a new `dict(...)` to the `I` list in `_tools/gen_industries.py`, following the existing entries exactly (same keys: slug, name, short, forname, biz, who, title, desc, h1, lead, card, takeaways, pains_h, pains_a, pains, fix_h, fix_a, fixes, journey, fit, faq, rel).
   - `title` ≤ 60 characters with the main buyer search phrase (e.g. "AI Receptionist & Lead Follow-Up for Chiropractors"); `desc` 140–160 characters.
   - `h1`: the industry's sharpest pain as an outcome ("Never miss a new-patient call again" style). It must differ from the other pages' headlines.
   - `pains`: 4 problems specific to that industry, in its own words. `fixes`: 6 solutions, each tagged with the service key it belongs to (`ai`, `ga`, `fb`, `lg`).
   - `journey`: 5 concrete steps of one realistic customer, specific to the industry.
   - `faq`: 3 questions buyers in that industry actually ask (compliance, "will customers know it's AI", software fit). Be accurate about regulations (e.g. HIPAA for healthcare, state insurance/mortgage advertising rules, NMLS disclosures for mortgage). Never claim certifications or compliance guarantees.
   - `rel`: 2 service keys + up to 1 relevant blog post key from `POSTS`.
   - **Never invent statistics, client names, case results or quotes.**
2. Run `python3 _tools/gen_industries.py`.
3. Homepage `index.html`:
   - Add a chip link to `<div class="who">` in the "Built for businesses like yours" tile.
   - In the industries live demo (`id="demo"`): if the industry is already one of its picker tiles (roofing `roof`, flooring `floor`, insurance `insure`, fitness `fitness`), change that `<button class="pick">` into `<a class="pick" href="industries/<slug>/" data-k="...">` (keep the icon and label). Don't add new tiles; the demo is limited to 9 high-value industries.
4. `sitemap.xml`: add `https://iamaftabahmed.com/industries/<slug>/` (lastmod today) and update the `/industries/` lastmod.
5. `llms.txt`: add a line under `## Industries` in the same format.
6. `blog/WRITING_GUIDE.md`: add the new page to the list of industry pages posts should link to.
7. `blog/topics.md`: write 4 new blog topics for that industry (problem-focused searches its owners make, e.g. "how to reduce no-shows at a chiropractic clinic | chiropractic no shows") and insert them into the Queue spaced about 9 lines apart, starting around line 3 of the unchecked queue. Don't duplicate existing topics.
8. Tick the industry in the queue above (`- [x]`).

## B. Refresh (one existing page)

Pick the industry page that was refreshed least recently (track it in the log below). Then:
1. Link its newest relevant blog posts: add them to `POSTS` and put their keys in that industry's `rel` list (keep the 2 service keys + up to 3 newest posts).
2. Improve one thing: add or sharpen an FAQ based on searches people make, make a vague line more specific, or update anything that has changed on the platforms (Google, Meta, etc.).
3. Re-run `python3 _tools/gen_industries.py`, update that page's lastmod in `sitemap.xml`, and add a line to the log.

## Finish

- Test every page with the Playwright check (no horizontal scroll at 390px wide, one H1, valid JSON-LD, no broken internal links). Fix anything that fails.
- Commit (`Industry page: <name>` or `Refresh industry page: <name>`) and push to the working branch **and** `main` (`git push origin HEAD:main`).
- IndexNow ping (best effort, skip if the network blocks it):
