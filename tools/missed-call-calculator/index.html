How is the cost of missed calls calculated?

The calculator multiplies five numbers you control: missed calls a month (calls per week × 4.33 × the share you miss), the share of those that are new potential customers, how many of those would become customers if someone answered, and what a new customer is worth. The result is revenue you are likely losing, not money that has already left your account, so treat it as an estimate.

The industry presets are example values to get you started, not industry averages. Your own call log and sales records will give a far more useful answer.

Where do I find my real numbers?

  • Calls and missed calls: your phone or VoIP provider's call log, or your call-tracking reports if you run ads.
  • New vs existing callers: listen to or sample a week of voicemails, or check how many missed numbers are not in your CRM.
  • Booking rate: of new callers your team does speak to, how many book or buy?
  • Customer value: average first job, or average yearly value for repeat services such as dental hygiene or HVAC maintenance.

How do businesses stop losing missed calls?

Answer faster, or reply instantly when you can't. The two most common fixes:

  • Missed call text back: every unanswered call gets a friendly text within seconds, so the caller has a reason not to dial the next business. See how missed call text back works.
  • AI receptionist: answers calls after hours or when lines are busy, handles routine questions and books appointments. See what an AI receptionist can do and how it compares with a live answering service.

Both work best with a follow-up sequence behind them; my guide to automated lead follow-up covers that. If you'd like it set up for you, see AI automation and AI receptionists.

FAQ

How do I find out how many calls I miss?

Most business phone systems and VoIP providers show answered and missed calls in a call log or report. Check a normal two-week period and divide by two for a weekly number. If you use call tracking for ads, it will show this too.

Are the default numbers industry averages?

No. The presets are example values to get you started. Replace them with your own numbers from your call log and your sales records for a result that means something for your business.

How much of the lost revenue can a missed-call text back or AI receptionist recover?

It depends on your callers and how fast and helpful the reply is. The recovery slider lets you test different assumptions. Start conservative, then measure real bookings for a month after setting it up.
