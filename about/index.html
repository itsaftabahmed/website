From design to systems

I spent 8+ years in design and marketing, delivering more than 1,500 projects for clients across the US, Europe and Asia. Over that time I kept seeing the same pattern: businesses invested in great-looking marketing, then lost leads because nobody replied fast enough, follow-ups slipped and teams were buried in repetitive admin.

So I moved into AI and automation. Today I build the whole system: the ads and pages that bring the right people in, and the AI assistants and automations that reply instantly, qualify leads and book appointments while the business owner gets on with their work.

What I do

  • AI automation & AI chatbots that answer, qualify and book leads 24/7
  • Google Ads management focused on calls and bookings, not just clicks
  • Facebook & Instagram ads with strong offers and instant follow-up
  • Lead generation systems: landing pages, CRM and speed-to-lead automation

Who I work with

Mostly medical practices, property businesses and accounting firms, plus growing service businesses that are losing time or leads to manual work. I'm based in Pakistan and work remotely with clients in the US, UK and Europe.

How I work

Three rules behind every project: no hype, speed over slowness and work before money. You get a clear plan first, small working pieces go live fast, and everything is handed over running and explained in plain English.
