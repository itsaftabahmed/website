Questions

Pricing questions

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Ad spend is never included in any plan. You pay Google or Meta directly for your ads, so you always own your ad accounts and see exactly what is spent. The monthly plan price covers the work, the tools and the management.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. The plan price includes your website, hosting, the CRM and normal texting and email use. The only separate cost is your ad spend, paid directly to Google or Meta. Anything extra, such as more locations, is quoted and agreed before it starts.

Is there a contract or minimum term?

Every plan starts with a 3-month minimum, because the website and system are built at no setup fee. After the first 3 months, plans run month to month and you can cancel any time with 30 days notice.

Who owns the website and accounts?

You do. Your website, domain, ad accounts and customer data stay in your name, even if you leave.

How long does SEO take to bring customers?

SEO usually takes 6 to 12 months to bring steady results from Google, depending on your market and competition. That is why every plan also includes tools that work in week one, such as instant lead alerts, follow-up and review requests.

I already have a website. Can I still use a plan?

Yes. On a free call we look at your current site. If it works well, we connect it to your CRM and follow-up; if it is holding you back, the new website is included in your plan.
