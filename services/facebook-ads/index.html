FAQ

Why do my Facebook ads get clicks but no calls?

Facebook ads usually get clicks but no calls because of what happens after the click: the offer, the landing page or form, or slow follow-up. Targeting is rarely the main problem. My full guide explains the six most common causes and how to fix them.

How much do Facebook ads cost for a small business?

Costs depend on your industry, location, audience and offer. Rather than chasing a cheap click, it's better to measure cost per qualified lead and cost per customer, then put more budget behind what works.

Should I use Facebook Lead Forms or a landing page?

Lead Forms usually produce more leads at a lower cost, while landing pages tend to produce fewer but more serious inquiries. A Lead Form with one or two qualifying questions plus instant follow-up is a strong starting point for many service businesses.
