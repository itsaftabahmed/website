FAQ

Will an AI chatbot sound robotic to my customers?

It shouldn't. The assistant is set up with your services, prices and tone of voice, and it is tested on real questions before launch. Anything it can't answer confidently is handed to a person.

Which tools do you use for AI automation?

It depends on what you already use. Common building blocks are a CRM, your booking calendar, your phone and SMS provider, and automation platforms such as Zapier, Make or n8n. The goal is to fit around your business, not force a new stack.

How long does it take to set up?

Simple setups such as missed-call text back or an FAQ assistant can be live quickly. A full system with qualification, booking and follow-up takes longer and is built in small, working pieces so you see value early.
