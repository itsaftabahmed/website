FAQ

What is a good cost per lead?

It depends on your industry and what a customer is worth to you. A better question is how much you can afford to pay for a customer, then working backwards to a cost per lead and booking rate that keeps that profitable.

How fast should I respond to a new lead?

As fast as possible, ideally within minutes. Interest drops quickly, and the first business to respond often wins. Automation makes instant replies possible even when your team is busy.

Do you generate leads or just set up the tools?

Both. I can run the ads and pages that bring leads in and build the follow-up system that turns them into appointments, or plug the follow-up system into the lead sources you already have.
