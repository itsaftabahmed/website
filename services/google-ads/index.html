FAQ

How much should a small business spend on Google Ads?

It varies a lot by industry and location, because click costs differ widely. A sensible approach is to start with a budget you can sustain for a few months, track cost per lead and cost per customer, and scale what works.

Google Ads or Facebook ads, which is better?

Google Ads captures people who are already searching for your service; Facebook and Instagram ads create demand among people who aren't searching yet. Many service businesses use Google for ready-to-buy demand and Facebook for awareness and retargeting.

Do I keep access to my Google Ads account?

Yes. Your ad account, data and tracking stay in your name. You always have full access.
