HVAC · Austin, Texas

Turning more inquiries into booked service calls

Scope: Google Ads, landing page, CRM and follow-up automation · Duration: 90 days

The challenge

The business received inquiries while its technicians were out on jobs. The owner handled callbacks between service visits, and new leads were scattered across email, voicemail and a spreadsheet.

The business needed a consistent process for responding to inquiries and recording which customers had booked.

The approach

The system connects Google Ads to a landing page focused on residential HVAC services. An inquiry form collects the service needed, ZIP code and preferred callback time.

Every submission creates a CRM record and sends an acknowledgment. The office receives a notification, while follow-up tasks flag inquiries awaiting contact. Staff confirm availability before booking a service visit.

Inquiry from ad→Acknowledgment sent→Office callback→Service call booked

What we tracked

  • First response time
  • Booked service calls
  • Weekly time spent on inquiry admin
Flooring · Charlotte, North Carolina

Helping homeowners take the next step

Scope: Meta Ads, landing pages, inquiry qualification and estimate follow-up · Duration: 12 weeks

The challenge

The business's ads generated messages, but staff often lacked the details needed to arrange an estimate. They repeatedly asked about flooring type, room size and location.

Some inquiries never progressed beyond the first conversation.

The approach

Separate landing pages explain hardwood installation and luxury vinyl flooring. Each page includes an inquiry form collecting basic project details and the homeowner's preferred consultation time.

New requests enter a CRM with a task for the estimator. A short acknowledgment explains the next step, and reminders help the team follow up with homeowners who have requested an estimate.

Landing page→Project details captured→Estimator task→Estimate scheduled

What we tracked

  • Scheduled estimates
  • Completeness of project details
  • Time spent on follow-up
Insurance · Columbus, Ohio

Bringing quote inquiries into one process

Scope: Inquiry forms, CRM integration and agent follow-up tasks · Duration: 10 weeks

The challenge

Quote inquiries arrived through website forms, ad campaigns and direct messages. Agents managed their own notes, making it difficult to identify unanswered requests.

The agency needed clearer ownership of each inquiry and a reliable record of follow-up activity.

The approach

All inquiry sources feed into a shared CRM. Requests are organized by the insurance category the prospect selects and assigned to an agent.

The system sends a receipt confirmation and creates follow-up tasks. Licensed agents remain responsible for discussing coverage, giving recommendations and preparing quotes.

New request→Assigned to agent→Contacted→Consultation booked

What we tracked

  • Inquiries contacted within one business day
  • Booked consultations
  • Unassigned requests
Dental · Raleigh, North Carolina

Making new-patient inquiries easier to manage

Scope: Administrative website assistant, appointment requests and front-desk notifications · Duration: 90 days

The challenge

The front desk balanced patient arrivals, phone calls and online inquiries. Prospective patients who contacted the practice outside office hours waited until staff returned.

The practice wanted to capture these requests consistently and reduce repeated administrative questions.

The approach

A website assistant answers practice-approved questions about opening hours, location and how to request an appointment. It captures minimal contact information and passes appointment requests to the front desk.

Staff review requests and confirm appointments. Questions about symptoms or treatment go directly to the practice team.

Question answered→Appointment request→Front desk review→Appointment confirmed

What we tracked

  • Confirmed appointments
  • Callback time
  • Time spent on administrative inquiries
Real estate · Tampa, Florida

Keeping property inquiries moving

Scope: Property landing pages, CRM routing and showing follow-up · Duration: 8 weeks

The challenge

The agency's agents received property inquiries through several channels. Requests were forwarded manually, and agents sometimes lacked a clear record of which listing a prospect was interested in.

The team needed a consistent handoff from first inquiry to agent conversation.

The approach

Property landing pages pass the listing reference and inquiry details into the CRM. Each request is assigned to an agent and triggers an acknowledgment.

Agents receive follow-up tasks, record contact attempts and arrange showings. Confirmed showing details feed into reminder messages.

Assigned→Contacted→Showing requested→Showing confirmed

What we tracked

  • Agent response time
  • Booked showings
  • Inquiries awaiting follow-up
Roofing · Denver, Colorado

Following up on estimate requests

Scope: Google Ads, estimate-request landing page and CRM automation · Duration: 12 weeks

The challenge

The owner managed estimate requests alongside site visits and project supervision. Requests often lacked essential details, and follow-up depended on handwritten notes.

The business needed an organized way to review requests and arrange inspections.

The approach

A focused landing page collects the property location, type of roofing request and preferred callback window. Optional photo uploads help staff understand the request before calling.

The CRM gives each inquiry a status and creates follow-up tasks. Inspection reminders are sent once staff confirm a time with the homeowner.

Estimate request→Details and photos→Callback→Inspection scheduled

What we tracked

  • Scheduled inspections
  • Same-day callbacks
  • Weekly time spent on inquiry admin
Accounting · Manchester, United Kingdom

Organizing consultation and onboarding requests

Scope: Consultation landing page, CRM and onboarding task automation · Duration: 90 days

The challenge

The firm received inquiries about bookkeeping, payroll and year-end accounts through a general contact form. Staff repeatedly had to clarify what each prospect needed before arranging a call.

After a prospect became a client, onboarding tasks were managed through separate email threads.

The approach

A consultation form collects business type, service interest and preferred contact time. Inquiries enter the CRM with tasks for the right team member.

Once the firm accepts a client, an onboarding checklist assigns responsibilities and sends instructions for submitting documents through the firm's secure portal.

Consultation request→Team member assigned→Client accepted→Onboarding checklist

What we tracked

  • Completeness of consultation requests
  • Onboarding turnaround
  • Administrative workload
Med spa · Scottsdale, Arizona

Helping inquiries become consultations

Scope: Meta Ads, consultation landing pages and inquiry follow-up · Duration: 10 weeks

The challenge

The clinic received interest through social media but struggled to keep track of consultation requests. Staff handled messages between appointments, and the next step was often unclear to prospective clients.

The clinic needed a consistent process for booking inquiries.

The approach

Campaigns send visitors to a consultation page that explains the booking process clearly. Inquiries enter a CRM and trigger an acknowledgment explaining when staff will respond.

Clinic staff handle suitability questions and confirm consultations. Reminders share appointment information once a booking is agreed.

Consultation request→Acknowledgment→Staff follow-up→Consultation confirmed

What we tracked

  • Confirmed consultations
  • First response time
  • Consultation attendance
Law firm · Phoenix, Arizona

Making initial inquiries easier to track

Scope: Initial inquiry forms, intake tracking and consultation scheduling · Duration: 90 days

The challenge

The firm received prospective-client inquiries through phone messages and its website. Staff copied details by hand into separate records, which made intake status hard to track.

The firm needed a consistent administrative process for reviewing requests and arranging the next step.

The approach

An initial inquiry form collects minimal contact details and a broad matter category, and steers people away from submitting confidential case details.

Requests enter an intake pipeline and create a staff review task. The firm handles conflict checks, suitability decisions and legal discussions before confirming the next step.

Received→Assigned→Under review→Consultation arranged

What we tracked

  • Inquiries reviewed within one business day
  • Staff assignment
  • Time spent entering details
Chiropractic · Nashville, Tennessee

Creating a clearer appointment-request journey

Scope: Google Ads, new-patient landing page and appointment-request follow-up · Duration: 12 weeks

The challenge

The practice's website attracted visitors but offered only a general contact form with little guidance about booking. The front desk had to clarify whether people wanted practice information or an appointment.

The practice needed a more direct inquiry journey and clearer tracking.

The approach

A new-patient landing page explains the appointment-request process and provides practice-approved administrative information.

Requests enter a central queue, trigger an acknowledgment and notify the front desk. Staff answer clinical questions and confirm availability. Confirmed bookings receive reminders.

Appointment request→Acknowledgment→Front desk follow-up→First visit confirmed

What we tracked

  • Appointment requests
  • Confirmed first appointments
  • First response time
